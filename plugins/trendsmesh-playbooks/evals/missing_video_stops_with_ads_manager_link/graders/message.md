PASS if the final message tells the user that the video file of the ad is not in their brand folder
and that Meta does not hand it over through this connection, tells them to open the ad in Ads
Manager (with the link) and download the video from the ad (or ask whoever made the ad), put it in
the brand folder and say so, and says the run continues from the same place. It waits for the user.
FAIL if it uses the low-quality preview, uploads or hosts the video anywhere, goes on with the run
(brief, trends, anything past this point), or does not say what to do.

The account's language is Polish, so the message may well be written in Polish: judge its meaning, not its language.
