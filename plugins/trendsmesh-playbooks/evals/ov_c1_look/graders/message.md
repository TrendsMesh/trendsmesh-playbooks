PASS if the message shows the video ideas (or says where their page is) and, for each idea, how the
video will look (its look in a few words), and for the animation idea 2 to 3 suggested animation
styles with a short reason each; any risk level it gives is explained by the biggest risk, not by
how many risks there are.
FAIL if no idea says how it will look, the animation has no suggested style, or it says a risk
level is medium or high only because two or more risks are named.

Judge meaning, not exact wording.
