PASS if the final message tells the user, in plain words, that the creative should NOT be refreshed
and gives the reason: the results of the whole account fell (a season, pixel or auction problem),
not just this ad, so a new video would not fix it. It may suggest looking at the account itself.
It must not promise or start a new video, a new hook or a live test, and must not show any command,
step id or file name as the main content.
FAIL if it recommends refreshing the ad, or does not say why it stops.

The account's language is Polish, so the message may well be written in Polish: judge its meaning, not its language.
