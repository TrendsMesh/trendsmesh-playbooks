PASS if the final message shows the user the choice of the direction of the changes to the
opening (hook) of the ad and waits for their answer, AND says plainly that without TrendsMesh
these directions are best guesses (from the account's own winners, the Ad Library or the agent's
judgement), not proof from what is trending or rising now.
FAIL if it presents any direction as backed by trend data or names specific trends, trend ids,
trend statuses or trend view counts; or if it refuses to go on, or tells the user they must sign
up or buy a plan before the run can continue; or if it does not offer a choice of direction.
Mentioning once that TrendsMesh would give better results, or that the Agent plan exists, is fine.

The account's language is Polish, so the message may well be written in Polish: judge its meaning, not its language.
