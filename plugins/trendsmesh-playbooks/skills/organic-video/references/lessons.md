# Lessons from the dry run (1 to 21)

Copied verbatim from the handoff's `ANALYSIS.md` (handoff `organic-video_v02`, section 6, the pet-store
dry run of 2026-11-04). They bind the agent: read the ones a step names before doing that step
(`flow.md`). What changed for the plugin:

- Lessons 1 and 2 (sound relevance, shared-word noise) are `tm trends shortlist --sound-relevance`
  and `--anchor-terms` (B2): the anchors are the channel's niche words.
- Lessons 3 to 6 and 20 (breed topics pick the cast, no format trends, hooks on screen at 0.0 s,
  no DIY topic, hook transfer) are read at B2 and C1: the format comes from the hook's evidence
  videos, and a transfer is marked (`plugin.transfer`, its weak link).
- Lesson 7 (a plan before anything is generated) is the pack check-in, "Approve the generation
  pack and its cost" (the AI video), and the script to film ($0).
- Lessons 8 to 19 and 21 are about generation, the edit and the look: they bind the AI video
  (`flow.md#e1` to `#g4`), and animation, quiz and ranking when those arrive. Lesson 11's prices are read live (never from this
  file); lesson 19's pending records are the engine's; lesson 21's style spec is OV3's.
- Where a lesson names `trend_shortlist.py`, `replicate_price.py` or `replicate_run.py`, the plugin
  runs `tm` commands; where it says "gate", read "check-in".

Run: `runs/03-organic-ai-video/2026-11-04_pet-dtc-dry-run/`.
1. **Sound shortlist has no niche relevance.** `trend_shortlist.py` ranks sounds by usability only, so the top sounds were generic (news audio, a game trailer, pop hits). Pet-fit sounds were found by matching pet terms in the sound's description and its example captions (e.g. *Masti Jaan Ke*, 222 creators; *Hi Hi Ha Ha!*, 61 creators). The tool needs a sound relevance score from `what_it_is` and the evidence captions.
2. **Topic noise from shared words:** "groom" and "brush" pulled human grooming (curly hair, eyebrows), "hack" pulled a hot-dog pasta hack. Relevance should require a niche anchor term (dog, pet, puppy) next to the activity term.
3. **Breed topics are grab-bags** ("Golden retriever videos": no shared activity). They pick the cast of the video, not its story. The story comes from the hook's evidence.
4. **No format trends yet** (`format_trend` = 0). Until they exist, the format is the structure of the hook's evidence videos.
5. **Hooks appear on screen at 0.0 s** in every evidence video with a timestamp (5 hooks checked, 82 videos; mostly on-screen text, some speech). Rule candidate: hook text on screen from the first frame.
6. **No DIY or hack topic for dogs** in the trend data; the hacks channel relies on hooks and sounds, with a breed topic as the cast.
7. **User (round 4): run the dry run as the SKILL will run it.** Before anything is generated, the user sees a plan per blend: the first frame of every shot, the model, parameters, prompts, gates and cost. Built as one **generation pack** per blend (`03_wip/packs/<blend>_pack_v01.json`): trends used, product presence, facts with sources, script beats, stages (product reference → character anchor and first frames → clips → edit), fallbacks, feasibility, posting kit. This is also the **Script output** for users without an AI stack (decision 6).
8. **Model prompting recipes** live in `resources/model-prompting/` (GPT Image 2, Veo 3.1), with sources and the check date; the agent reads the recipe before writing prompts.
9. **Consistency in 9:16:** on Replicate, Veo 3.1 `reference_images` work only in 16:9 (and not on Fast). For vertical video the consistency comes from first frames made with GPT Image 2 from one approved anchor image (`input_images`). Product reference images are made once per store and reused by every channel.
10. **No native audio for organic clips:** `generate_audio: false`, since the trending sound is attached in the app.
11. ~~Replicate model pages show no prices~~ **Wrong (user, round 5):** they do; the visible "$0.0001 per second" is a placeholder and the real price sits in `billingConfig`. `tools/replicate_price.py` reads it (Veo 3.1 Fast: $0.10/s without audio, $0.15/s with; GPT Image 2 medium: $0.047 per image; MiniMax H3: $0.08/s at 768P; checked 2026-11-04).
12. **Model choice by ranking (2026-11-04):** image-to-video leaders on Artificial Analysis (with audio) and Arena are Seedance 2.5/2.0, MiniMax H3, Wan 3.0, Grok Imagine 1.5; Veo 3.1 Fast ranks 22–24 (AA) and 15 (Arena). On Replicate, **MiniMax H3** (Arena #1, AA 3–7) costs $0.08/s at 768P, below Veo 3.1 Fast ($0.10/s). H3 also makes **up to 15 s with a timed shot list in one generation**, which keeps the dog, the light and the room consistent across shots. Images: GPT Image 2 (medium) is the top released model on the Arena image-edit board (#4 behind three previews).
13. **Stages 1–2 generated (2026-11-04, $0.47):** product references and first/last frames came out usable on the first try (10 images, no retakes). GPT Image 2 kept the product shape and color from `input_images` in every frame. Two process notes for the SKILL: (a) every image spec must set `output_format: png` (the G1 first frame came back as webp because the pack omitted it); (b) when the user approves several stages at once, the agent picks variants at the inner gates, marks them on a contact sheet (`showcase/`) and the user confirms before the next paid stage.
14. **MiniMax H3 takes (2026-11-04, $2.40, no retakes, ~5 min each):** both 15 s multi-shot takes kept the dog, the product and the room consistent across cuts; the brush and the mat match their packshots. Timing: G1 followed the shot list to the frame (cuts at 6.0 and 8.96 s); S1 cut the opening medium shot to **0.75 s** instead of 3 s (the opening shot = the first frame, and the model left it fast). Defects: in G1 the spatula vanishes between 3.3 and 4.2 s (the hand then presses a blueberry; fixable by trimming shot 1 at ~3.8 s in the edit); in S1 the brush is briefly shown with the hand off-frame (reads as natural). Rule candidate: when the opening shot must hold, say so ("hold this framing for 3 seconds before cutting") and check cut times with scene detection in QA.
15. **Default safe zone (decision 12), set 2026-11-04:** at 1080x1920 keep text and key action inside **x 90–880, y 260–1540** (margins: top 260, bottom 380, left 90, right 200). Built as the union of the strictest published figures: the cross-platform 900x1400 centred zone (top 260), Reels top 210, TikTok bottom 320 / right 120, Shorts right column ~18.6% (~200 px) and bottom band ~19.8% (~380 px). The platforms publish no pixel boxes; the figures are third-party (reap.video, upload-post, adaptlypost, admakeai) and must be checked in the platform's own preview at the first real post. Implemented in `tools/remotion/src/OrganicCaptions.tsx` (`SAFE_1080`, `showSafeZone` for QA stills).
16. **Edit (free):** Remotion composition `OrganicCaptions`: source segments cut back to back, always muted, captions in two styles (hook = white box with black text; note = white text with a dark outline), anchored top / middle / bottom inside the safe zone, `\n` for deliberate line breaks. QA: stills with the safe-zone overlay before the render, then a 1 fps strip of the final. Two fixes in v01: awkward wraps ("(day / 2)", "(no / xylitol)"), solved with shorter text and explicit line breaks. A step-by-step number without a source ("freeze 2–3 hours") was replaced by "freeze until firm" (R3).
17. **W1, animation (the old library's style 01, 9:16, first run of the `--aspect 9:16` mode), $0:** a subagent built it in 3 versions (~25 min, ~310k tokens of Claude usage): verbatim on-screen text, centred, inside the safe zone, question readable at t = 0, no page errors. Problems left: the style's signature **space backdrop (planets, a planet under the dog) leaks into a dog topic**; one tiny label ("Cracks") under the size rule; ~0.2 s without text near 19 s. Rule candidates: the brief must say which parts of the reference style are setting (to replace with the topic's setting) and which are rendering (to keep); the storyboard gate was skipped for a free animation, but a 1-panel style frame check would have caught the backdrop before 3 full builds.
18. **R1 ranking (countdown 5 → 1), $2.15:** five first frames from the S1 anchor + five 4–5 s H3 takes + Remotion `RankingList` (title block with an accent line and a teaser, numbered list on the left that fills as each clip starts, active number in the accent colour). The format reads like the evidence. The husky's coat shade varies between takes (each take starts from its own frame); #4 "the sigh" is the weakest motion. Overlay QA caught a 5-line title over the list and a number touching its label (fixed: 50 px title, nowrap; wider number column).
19. **Paid predictions must survive client errors:** all five R1 takes succeeded on Replicate while every local `pred.wait()` died (WinError 10054 / read timeout); the files were recovered from the predictions list. `tools/replicate_run.py` now writes `<name>.pending.json` with the prediction id right after creating it and polls with retries.
20. **Hook transfer across subjects:** "dad, why is _" has evidence only in sports. Using it for pets is a hypothesis; the blend says so and it lowers expected reach, not feasibility.
21. **Reproducing a format means reproducing its look, not only its structure (user round 8, 2026-11-05):** R1 v01 copied the anatomy (countdown, list on the left, labels landing) but used our own caption look (all caps, one orange accent, list over a full-screen clip), and the user rejected it as not looking like the found rankings. v02 (`RankingSplit`) copies the visual system read from the evidence thumbnails: split screen with a blurred zoom of the clip on top carrying the text, the clip below; rounded bold sans in Title Case; a different colour per key word (yellow, red, blue, white) with a thick black outline; small green teaser; italic numbers in medal colours (1 gold, 2 silver, 3 bronze, 4–5 white) with no active highlight; white label + emoji + grey score. Rule for the SKILL: when a run reproduces a format, the blend states a **style spec** taken from at least two evidence videos (layout, font family class, colours, number style, extras such as emoji) and shows it side by side with the reference before rendering. The right-hand 200 px margin of the safe zone guards the action-button column, which sits in the lower half; text in the top panel may use the full width inside the 90 px side margins (top margin 260 kept). To verify with the platform previews together with the other safe-zone values.
