## TikTok
- **Caption:** hook + search phrase in line 1; optionally one genuine question; soft product line.
- **Hashtags:** 3–5 specific ones.
- **Cover:** frame <s> + 2–4 words (the engine accepts 2–7, as the handoff's approved kits).
- **AI label:** on for photoreal AI; mandatory for an AI human host.
- **Sound:** original kept, or a trending sound added in the app (name, evidence link, steps, original 100% / added 10–15%). For business accounts: a commercial-library alternative.
- **Product:** “Your brand” disclosure when the product is shown.

## Instagram Reels
- **Caption:** first line ≤ 125 characters.
- **Hashtags:** ≤ 5.
- **Cover image:** safe for the 3:4 grid crop.
- **AI info:** on for photoreal AI.
- **Sound.**
- **Optional:** a Shop tag.

## YouTube Shorts
- **Title:** ≤ 100 characters, hook + keyword.
- **Description:** 1–2 sentences + 2–3 hashtags.
- **AI use:** Yes for photoreal.
- **Product:** links are not clickable in Shorts, so it goes on the channel page.
- **Thumbnail:** optional (tick on the final page; a generated thumbnail is not supported yet).

## Facebook Reels
- **Caption:** short.
- **Hashtags:** 1–3.
- **Cover.**
- **AI info.**

Never: sources, engagement bait (“comment X”, “tag a friend”), keyword stuffing, product as the lead.
