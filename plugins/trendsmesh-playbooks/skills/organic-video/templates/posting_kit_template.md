## TikTok
- **Description to paste (with hashtags):** hook + search phrase in line 1; optionally one genuine question; soft product line; then 3–5 specific hashtags. At most 2,200 characters as pasted.
- **At posting:** the cover (frame <s> + 2–7 words); the AI label (on for photoreal AI, on every platform; mandatory for an AI human host; off for a clearly animated style unless the user asks); the sound (original kept, or a trending sound added in the app: name, evidence link, steps, original 100% / added 10–15%; for business accounts a commercial-library alternative); “Your brand” disclosure when the product is shown; check the search phrase in Creator Search Insights.

## Instagram Reels
- **Description to paste (with hashtags):** first line ≤ 125 characters; at most 5 hashtags; at most 2,200 characters and 20 @ tags.
- **At posting:** a cover image safe for the 3:4 grid crop; AI info as on TikTok; the sound; optionally a Shop tag.

## YouTube Shorts
- **Title:** ≤ 100 characters, hook + keyword, no < or >.
- **Description (with hashtags):** 1–2 sentences + 2–3 hashtags, at most 5,000 bytes.
- **Tags:** a few searched phrases (the topic, the search phrase, a broader one), at most 500 characters as YouTube counts them.
- **At posting:** AI use = Yes for photoreal; the product goes on the channel page (links are not clickable in Shorts); thumbnail optional (tick on the final page; a generated thumbnail is not supported yet).

## Facebook Reels
- **Description to paste (with hashtags):** short, 1–3 hashtags of one word each.
- **At posting:** the cover; AI info as on Instagram.

Never: links, sources, engagement bait (“comment X”, “tag a friend”), keyword stuffing, product as the lead.
