## Concept page (one per concept)
Order: short summary first, details after (user, 2026-11-04). The reader must see at the top of each concept what it is, what it tests and **which data back it**, before any beat sheet.

0. **Evidence at a glance** (right under the idea and the hypothesis, one compact block, every number with its source):
   - **Trend (TrendsMesh):** the trend the concept stands on, labelled by type ("Rising hook", "Rising topic", "Rising format", "Rising sound" + licence): name and ID, status, momentum, creators, views, median view lift, active since / last seen, language of the evidence;
   - **Customers:** the theme, its tier and frequency ("74 of 864 items");
   - **Account (Meta):** the number that makes this concept relevant (segment, placement, baseline cost to beat);
   - **Competition (Ad Library):** "competitor found" or "none found in Ad Library titles", never "hook unused"; with Foreplay: in paid N ads, longest running D days (CB4).
1. **Idea in one sentence** and the test hypothesis ("this concept tests whether …").
2. **Why customers care (angle):** theme, tier (A own customers / B category / C brand), frequency ("x of y items"), 2–3 verbatim quotes with source.
3. **Why now (trend):** trend card in short (what it is, formula, status, since, creators, views, view lift), 3 evidence clips (thumbnail + link + "as of" date), what exactly we take from it (structure, slot_fill, text position and timing).
4. **Hook:** verbatim on-screen text and first spoken line, what the viewer sees during it, hook-to-image relation (consistent / setup and payoff / deliberate contradiction + reason).
5. **Structure in 4–6 beats** (seconds): hook → … → CTA.
6. **Saturation and benchmark:** the CB4 verdict for the market with the relevant advertisers found; Foreplay benchmark if connected.
6a. **Customers' own words:** at least one language-bank phrase used in the hook, on-screen text, a line or the primary text, with where it is used and its source (CB5).
7. **Sound:** none / licensed track / sound trend with its licensed equivalent or "organic only" (CB9).
8. **Fit score** table (CB3) with one line of reasoning per criterion.

## Vote (Check every step, Just the big calls)
Per concept: go / no / change (with a note). Hands-off: top N by score that pass the diversity check.
