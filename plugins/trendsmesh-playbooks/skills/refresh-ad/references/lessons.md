# Lessons from the dry run (1 to 13) and the re-cut tests (14 to 17)

Lessons 1 to 13 are copied verbatim from the handoff's `PLAYBOOK.md` (handoff v02, "Lessons from the
dry run (to be built into the SKILL)"). Lessons 14 to 17 come from the re-cut tests
(handoff v03: `PLAYBOOK.md` R9, `FLOWS.md` path C-R, `VARIANTS.md` 3). Read the ones a step names
before doing that step. Two of them changed
for the plugin: the file never comes from the Meta MCP (lesson 1, see `meta-mcp.md`, A2), and
upload never needs public hosting (lesson 8: we never host files publicly; see E2 in `flow.md`).

1. **Winner file:** Meta MCP does not return the video source (`download_hd_url` = null), so the file has to be taken from the brand folder or requested. Mandatory input.
2. **Burned-in captions in the video:** the hook swap covers the old frames with new cards. Frame positions and timings are detected automatically (OpenCV); it works, but clean sources without captions would give better quality.
3. **No speech in the ad:** transcription may return empty. The structure is then based on captions and shots.
4. **Ad Library via API** returns only titles, so a browser or Foreplay is needed for a structure benchmark.
5. **Trends come mostly from EN TikTok:** transferring them to PL is a hypothesis. Rank by number of creators and view lift, not by views alone.
6. **Hook quality:** text from the first frame (the original had an empty frame for 1.3 s), large font. Show the product before the CTA.
7. **Replicate prices** must be confirmed before running (the pricing page cannot be read automatically).
8. **Upload to Meta:** `ads_creative_upload_media` accepts a local file (interactively) or a public URL. AUTO mode needs hosting with a public URL.
9. **Every change has a justification in the data.** Every output has a change register (change → data → expected effect). In HITL mode the agent shows it before acceptance. Changes beyond the hook are allowed, but must have a justification.
10. **Hook fidelity:** before writing the text, the agent analyzes the trend's example videos (slot_fill, sub-patterns, place and time of appearance) and reproduces the structure as closely as possible. Otherwise the test does not check the trend, only our interpretation.
11. **Hook-to-image consistency (mandatory gate in step 6).** For each candidate the agent records what the viewer sees during the hook and afterwards. The relation of the text to the image must be one of three: (a) **consistent**: the text describes the state on screen, same time and state; (b) **teaser and punchline**: the text announces a goal, and the video first shows the "before" state, then the "after"; (c) **deliberate contradiction**: irony with a recorded justification. Text in the future tense ("aż polecę" ("until I fly")) over a shot that already shows the result is rejected.
12. **Covering burned-in captions:** we change only the captions that actually change and leave the rest alone. The card is static and spans the whole life cycle of the old frame: from the first frame of the entry animation to the last, with a margin of a few frames and pixels. QA checks the **geometry** (whether the card covers the frame outline in every frame), not pixel color, because a sunset produces false alarms.
    - **An overlay is not just a frame and text.** We also look for semi-transparent elements (bars, shadows, backgrounds) using edge detection or temporal differences, not color thresholds. Version v04 let through a semi-transparent white bar that slid in from the left edge.
    - **Edge rule:** if the overlay touches the frame edge in any frame (or comes closer than about 24 px), the card goes edge to edge (`fullBleed`).
    - **Visual QA:** enlarged strips of the card's left and right edges for the first and last frames of the overlay cycle (`03_wip/qa/*_edges.png`). In HITL mode we show them to the human.
13. **AI clip chain (Veo 3.1 Fast, max 8 s):** each next clip starts from the exact last frame of the previous one. We use `last_frame` only when the end frame is close to the start frame (same scene, light and elements). In the dry run an end frame with a different composition caused background morphing and the moon disappearing in the middle of clip 2. We change the setting with a camera move described in the prompt (e.g. a fly-over along a ridge). Join QA: the difference between the last and first frame is smaller than the median of differences between frames within the clip, plus a search for jumps (> 1.7× the median) assessed on frame strips.
14. **The sibilant cut (PLAYBOOK.md R9, 2026-11-04):** when cutting words out of a take, do not trust the TTS word-start timestamp for words that begin with a sibilant (s, sh, z, f), because it lands late. Place the cut in the first silence (< −55 dBFS for 30 ms) after the last kept word, then check that no burst is left after it. On 2026-11-04 a cut before "Sixteen" left the "S" as an audible hiss.
15. **The one-frame flash (FLOWS.md C-R, ad_2 J1 v04):** the 30 fps conversion can pull the first frame of the next shot into a segment that ends within about a frame of a shot change. The user did not notice it in J1 v04 (the segment ended at 19.80 s, the shot changes at 19.798 s); the QA check did, and J1 v05 ends at 19.76 s. A segment ends at least 2 frames before a shot change, counted in the source's frames (`tm recut points`, `tm recut check`), and the render's joint strips are looked at.
16. **Lips behind the audio (VARIANTS.md 3, ad_2 J1 v01):** a plain crossfade overlap at a speech joint shifts the audio ahead of the lips by its length at every joint (+60 ms per joint in v01). Speech is cut in sync mode (`audio_mode: "sync"`: picture and sound cut together, a 40 ms overlap-add that does not shorten), never with a plain crossfade or a dissolve.
17. **Caption flash (FLOWS.md CR6, ad_2 J1 v01 to v03):** burned word captions switch about 0.1 to 0.2 s before or after the speech, so a picture cut in the speech gap can leave a word of the cut-out speech on screen for 2 to 3 frames ("próbowałam"). Cut the picture at the caption switch and let the audio run on into the neighbour's silence (`audio_tail` L-cut, `audio_head` J-cut); QA flags any caption shown less than 0.2 s next to a joint (`tm tool recut_qa --caption-band`).
