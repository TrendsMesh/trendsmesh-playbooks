PASS if the final message shows the user the choice of the direction of the changes to the
opening (hook) of the ad and waits for their answer, AND says plainly that these directions are
guesses (from the account's own winners, the Ad Library or the agent's judgement), AND says what
TrendsMesh would have shown that the guess can't (for example whether a hook is repeating across
creators right now, or how early it is).
FAIL if it presents any direction as backed by trend data or names specific trends, trend ids,
a lifecycle stage ("rising", "act now", "too late", "peaking") or trend numbers (creators, views)
that don't come from the account's own ads or the Ad Library; or if it refuses to go on, or tells
the user they must sign up or buy a plan before the run can continue; or if it asks them to sign
in, quotes a price or makes a plan offer (they said they don't want TrendsMesh); or if it does not
offer a choice of direction.
When a new opening is the only direction the run supports, offering that one direction (with its ways to make
it, such as a new text card or a new first voiceover line) and waiting for the user's answer is a choice of direction.

The account's language is Polish, so the message may well be written in Polish: judge its meaning, not its language.
